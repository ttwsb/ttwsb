Who We Are

About TTWSB

Our Story

Built by trainers, for trainers

The TVET Trainers' Welfare Society of Bangladesh (TTWSB) was formed by working instructors at Technical Training Centres (TTCs) and vocational institutes who saw the need for a unified, government-registered body to represent their profession. TTWSB is officially registered under Reg. No. RAJS-616/2026 and is governed by a 5-member Advisory Panel and an elected 21-member Executive Committee (2026–2029 term).

Since its founding, TTWSB has grown into a nationwide network connecting trainers across all divisions — sharing resources, advocating for fair recognition, and supporting members through welfare programmes.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To unite, represent and support TVET trainers across Bangladesh, promoting professional recognition and welfare.

Vision

A Bangladesh where every technical and vocational trainer is professionally recognised, fairly supported, and connected to a strong national community.

Our Objectives

What TTWSB works toward

  1. Represent TVET trainers' interests to government and education authorities.
  2. Maintain a verified national register of member trainers.
  3. Provide welfare and emergency assistance to members and their families.
  4. Organise training, workshops and knowledge-sharing events.
  1. Advocate for fair compensation and working conditions for trainers.
  2. Build partnerships with technical institutes across Bangladesh.
  3. Promote continuous professional development within the trade.
  4. Strengthen a nationwide community of practice among trainers.
