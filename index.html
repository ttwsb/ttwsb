About TTWSB

A collective voice for technical & vocational trainers

TTWSB was formed by working TVET trainers to strengthen professional recognition, share resources, and provide welfare support to instructors serving at government and private technical training centres across the country. Every member holds a verifiable digital ID recognised nationwide.
