Membership

Eligibility

Who can apply

Membership is open to any currently working TVET trainer/instructor at a government or private Technical Training Centre (TTC), Polytechnic, or vocational institute in Bangladesh.

  • ✓ Currently employed as a TVET trainer/instructor
  • ✓ Valid National ID (NID)
  • ✓ Recommendation / employment proof from institute
  • ✓ Agreement to the society's constitution
